In the modern e-commerce stack, User-Generated Content (UGC) and Loyalty Programs are often treated as separate silos. One captures the voice of the customer (Ratings, Reviews, Q&A); the other manages the economy of retention. For brands and retailers, the challenge isn’t just “collecting reviews”—it’s building a high-performance bridge between user action and value generation.

Shoppable video and TikTok Shop are reshaping product discovery by bringing inspiration, product proof, and checkout closer together. Shoppers can now watch a creator demo, tap a product tag, read reviews or comments, and buy while interest is still high. TikTok Shop is one of the most visible examples.

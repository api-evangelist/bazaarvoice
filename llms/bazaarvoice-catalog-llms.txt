# Bazaarvoice

> Bazaarvoice operates a retail and brand user-generated-content network - ratings, reviews, questions and answers, and product sampling - syndicated across thousands of brand and retailer sites. Its developer platform exposes the Conversations, Content Search, Transactions, Response, Social Commerce, and Authentic Discovery APIs for retrieving and submitting UGC and structured data.

## APIs
- [Bazaarvoice Content Search API](openapi/bazaarvoice-content-search-openapi.json): Search reviews, questions, answers, contributors, catalog lookahead, and structured data (JSON-LD/Microdata).
